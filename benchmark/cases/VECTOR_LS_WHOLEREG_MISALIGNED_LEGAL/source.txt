Source: RISC-V Unprivileged ISA Manual, unpriv/vector-common.adoc (whole register loads/stores)

Implementations are allowed to raise a misaligned address exception on
whole register loads and stores if the base address is not naturally
aligned to the larger of the size of the encoded EEW in bytes (EEW/8) or
the implementation's smallest supported SEW size in bytes (SEW_MIN/8).
